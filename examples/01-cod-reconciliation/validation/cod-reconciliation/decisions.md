## 2026-10-19 — Experiment 02: Pre-sale to shops → **CHANGE**

**Question:** A4 — shops pay $15/month for automatic reconciliation.
**Bar (set on 2026-10-12):** success ≥ 5 of ~15 deposits; kill ≤ 2 of ~15.
**What happened:** 1 of 15 qualified shops paid (L5). Kill line hit. Combined with experiment 01: shops spend time on reconciliation (6 of 8, L2) but rarely lose money from it (1 of 8, L1).
**Verdict:** CHANGE — keep the problem, change the paying segment to small courier agencies, who carry the cost of disputes.
**What we are NOT concluding:** that agencies will pay. The agency signal is 3 unplanned conversations (L1) and 2 L0 mentions — enough to choose the next test, not to build.
**Strongest case for the opposite verdict:** A lower price ($5) might convert shops; time saved is real. But shops described the spreadsheet as "fine", and a lower price makes the economics fail before acquisition costs. One price test is not worth more than a test of the segment that actually loses money.
**Assumption status now:** A4 → contradicted (L5). A2 → contradicted for shops (L1). New A9 → untested, next in exp 03.
**Next step:** Experiment 03 — offer a paid concierge pilot ($60/month, weekly reconciliation done by hand) to 6 courier agencies, by 2026-10-30.
**Validation debt:** 3 critical assumptions without L3+ evidence (A1, A5, A9)

## 2026-10-12 — Experiment 01: Problem interviews with shops → **CHANGE**

**Question:** A1 — shops spend ≥ 2 h/week reconciling; A2 — errors cost money monthly.
**Bar (set on 2026-10-05):** success ≥ 5 of 8 show an artifact and ≥ 4 of 8 name a loss; kill ≤ 2 of 8 show an artifact; between the lines → change the segment or the problem framing.
**What happened:** 6 of 8 showed a weekly spreadsheet (L2); 1 of 8 named a loss (L1). Between the lines.
**Verdict:** CHANGE the problem framing — from "errors cost you money" to "this costs you 2–3 hours every week". Test whether that time is worth paying for with a pre-sale.
**What we are NOT concluding:** that shops lose money (A2 missed its line) or that they'll pay for saved time.
**Strongest case for the opposite verdict:** Changing the segment now (to the courier agencies who described real losses) might be the better single change. Counter: the agency signal is 3 unplanned conversations, and a shop pre-sale costs only 6 hours and gives an L5 answer on A4 directly.
**Assumption status now:** A1 → supported (L2); A2 → contradicted for shops (L1); A9 added from the agency conversations.
**Next step:** Experiment 02 — pre-sale to shops, 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-18.
**Validation debt:** 4 critical assumptions without L3+ evidence (A1, A4, A5, A9)
