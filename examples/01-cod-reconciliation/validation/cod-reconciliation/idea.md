# Cash-on-delivery reconciliation

> Started: 2026-10-05 · Owner: two-person team (product + developer) · Time budget: 8 h/week until 2026-11-30

## In one sentence
Owners of small online shops who sell through Instagram and messengers and ship with third-party couriers can see, every evening, which orders were paid and which courier owes them what — instead of reconciling a spreadsheet against courier chats by hand. `guess`

## Who exactly
Shops with 20–150 orders a week, 1–3 people, selling clothes, cosmetics or home goods, using one or two courier services with cash on delivery. Tashkent and Almaty first. `guess`

## What they do today instead
A Google Sheet or Excel file updated by hand from courier chat messages and bank transfers; some call couriers directly. `inference` (from two conversations before starting)

## Why now, why us
- Cash on delivery is still common for small shops in the region. `guess`
- One of us worked in courier operations and knows how payouts and disputes happen. `fact`

## What would make this worth it
20 paying shops by the end of January, or a clear STOP by the end of November. `fact` (team decision)

## Constraints
No integrations with courier services in the first version. No handling of customers' money. `fact`
