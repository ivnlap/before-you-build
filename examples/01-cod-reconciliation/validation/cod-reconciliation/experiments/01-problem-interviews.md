# Experiment 01 — Problem interviews with shops

> Status: DONE · Created: 2026-10-05 · Runs: 2026-10-06 → 2026-10-11

## What we're testing
Assumption A1: Shops with 20–150 orders/week spend ≥ 2 h/week reconciling COD payments.
Assumption A2: Reconciliation errors cost shops money at least monthly.

## Hypothesis
We believe that owners of small COD shops will describe a recent, specific reconciliation session and a recent loss caused by an error.

## The test
Type: Problem interviews · Budget: $0 · Effort: ~8 h
Steps:
1. DM 30 shops found through seller chats and Instagram hashtags.
2. Hold 8 calls of 25 minutes using the byb-interview script.
3. Ask each to show the spreadsheet they use.

## Pre-registered bar (do not edit after the start date)
- Measure: in-segment shops interviewed (target 8)
- Success line: ≥ 5 of 8 show a weekly reconciliation artifact (A1) **and** ≥ 4 of 8 name a specific loss in the last 3 months (A2) → CONTINUE to a pre-sale
- Kill line: ≤ 2 of 8 show an artifact → STOP
- Between the lines: CHANGE the segment or the problem framing
- Evidence level this can reach: L2
- Minimum sample before deciding: 6 in-segment interviews

## If it passes / fails
- Pass → pre-sale to shops (experiment 02).
- Fail → re-examine who feels the loss.

## Result
- Raw numbers: 30 DMs → 11 replies → 8 calls held, all in segment. 6 of 8 showed a weekly spreadsheet (avg. reported 2–3 h/week). 1 of 8 named a specific loss (≈ $40 in September).
- Notes: 3 shops introduced us to their courier agencies; all 3 agency owners described monthly disputes with shops over cash, one estimating 1–2 days a month of a manager's time.
- Bar was changed after start? no
