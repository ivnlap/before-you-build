# Experiment 02 — Pre-sale to shops

> Status: DONE · Created: 2026-10-12 · Runs: 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-18

## What we're testing
Assumption A4: Shops pay $15/month for automatic reconciliation.

## Hypothesis
We believe that qualified shops (20–150 orders/week, COD) will pay a refundable $15 deposit for the first month when offered a reconciliation tool launching on 2026-11-20.

## The test
Type: Pre-sale / deposit · Budget: $0 · Effort: ~6 h
Steps:
1. One-page offer: what it does, price $15/month, launch date, full refund if not delivered.
2. Send to the 8 interviewed shops + 20 new qualified shops; follow up once.
3. Count deposits paid by bank transfer.

## Pre-registered bar (do not edit after the start date)
- Measure: deposits paid / qualified shops who read the offer (confirmed reply or call)
- Success line: ≥ 5 of ~15 → CONTINUE to build the first version
- Kill line: ≤ 2 of ~15 → CHANGE or STOP
- Between the lines: test a lower price or a different offer once
- Evidence level this can reach: L5
- Minimum sample before deciding: 12 shops who confirmed they read the offer

## If it passes / fails
- Pass → build the spreadsheet-import version for the paying shops.
- Fail → decide between price, offer and segment.

## Result
- Raw numbers: offer sent to 28 → 15 confirmed reading it → 1 paid the deposit. 4 said "maybe later", 2 said the spreadsheet is "fine".
- Notes: two shops asked whether their courier agency could pay for it instead.
- Bar was changed after start? no
