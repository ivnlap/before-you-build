# Experiment 01 — Problem interviews with tutors

> Status: DONE · Created: 2026-09-15 · Runs: 2026-09-16 → 2026-09-20

## What we're testing
Assumption A1: Private tutors with 10+ students lose money or ≥ 2 h a month to scheduling and unpaid lessons.

## Hypothesis
We believe that tutors with 10+ students will describe a specific recent case of a lost lesson fee or hours spent rescheduling.

## The test
Type: Problem interviews · Budget: $0 · Effort: ~6 h
Steps:
1. Recruit through two tutor groups and a tutoring marketplace (not friends).
2. Hold 10 calls of 20 minutes using the byb-interview script; no pitching.
3. Ask each to describe the last reschedule and the last late payment.

## Pre-registered bar (do not edit after the start date)
- Measure: in-segment tutors (10+ students) interviewed
- Success line: ≥ 6 of 10 describe a specific loss of money or ≥ 2 h in the last month → CONTINUE
- Kill line: ≤ 3 of 10 → STOP unless a clear adjacent signal appears
- Between the lines: CHANGE the segment (e.g. small tutoring centres)
- Evidence level this can reach: L1–L2
- Minimum sample before deciding: 8 in-segment interviews

## If it passes / fails
- Pass → fake door with an $8/month price.
- Fail → stop, unless an adjacent group clearly shows the pain.

## Result
- Raw numbers: 26 messages → 12 replies → 10 calls held, all in segment. 2 of 10 described a specific loss in the last month (one unpaid lesson, ≈ $15; one ~3 h of rescheduling). 7 of 10 said calendar + messenger "works fine". 1 of 10 pays for a scheduling tool.
- Notes: 4 of 10 raised finding new students as their real headache, unprompted.
- Bar was changed after start? no
