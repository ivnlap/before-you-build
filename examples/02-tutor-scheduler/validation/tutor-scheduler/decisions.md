## 2026-09-21 — Experiment 01: Problem interviews with tutors → **STOP**

**Question:** A1 — tutors with 10+ students lose money or ≥ 2 h a month to scheduling and unpaid lessons.
**Bar (set on 2026-09-15):** success ≥ 6 of 10; kill ≤ 3 of 10 unless a clear adjacent signal appears.
**What happened:** 2 of 10 described a specific recent loss (L1). 7 of 10 are satisfied with calendar + messenger; 1 of 10 pays for any tool. Desk scan: the market already has many free tools (L2 desk).
**Verdict:** STOP — the core problem isn't painful enough for this segment, and free alternatives are good enough.
**What we are NOT concluding:** that tutors have no problems worth solving. 4 of 10 raised finding new students — that's a different idea with its own assumptions, not a pivot of this one.
**Strongest case for the opposite verdict:** Tutors with 25+ students or small tutoring centres might feel the pain more (CHANGE segment). But none of the 10 conversations pointed there, the two who reported losses had 12 and 14 students, and the market is crowded with free tools for that segment too. Testing it would be a new bet without a signal.
**Assumption status now:** A1 → contradicted (L1); A3 → contradicted (L1). Idea archived.
**Next step:** Archive. If interested, start a new idea folder for "finding new students" with `byb-start`, reusing the 10 contacts.
**Validation debt:** 0 — idea stopped. Time spent: ~9 h. Avoided: ~2 months of building.
